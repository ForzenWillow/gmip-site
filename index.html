The problem

When shoppers can’t find it, your store loses.

For visually impaired and elderly customers, an unfamiliar layout turns a routine shop into a stressful, time-consuming task. That cost lands on the shopper, your staff and your bottom line.

01

Lower satisfaction from wasted time

Shoppers walk past the right aisle again and again, struggle to read packaging and leave tired and anxious.

Fewer completed baskets, weaker loyalty
02

Customers avoid unfamiliar stores

If a store is hard to navigate, people stick with the one they already know — or stop shopping in person at all.

New customers lost to competitors
03

Staff are pulled off the floor

Every “where can I find…?” interrupts restocking and other essential tasks, at a time when stores are short of people.

Lower productivity, slower restocking

Our solution

Your store map, made accessible.

GMIP turns your floor plan and product locations into guidance your customers can hear, feel and see. The app adapts to each shopper’s level of visual impairment.

Audio guidance

Step-by-step spoken directions, from the entrance to each product and on to the checkout.

Haptic feedback

A clear vibration tells the shopper they’ve reached the right shelf. No need to look at the screen.

Visual maps

High-contrast maps with large text for shoppers with partial sight and older customers.

One app, three entry points Set once, and every visit opens in the shopper’s mode.

How it works

From floor plan to guided store.

Becoming a GMIP partner store takes four steps. We do the technical work; your team shares what it already knows about the store.

  1. 1

    Share your layout

    Send us your floor plan and product locations, straight from your existing planogram or inventory systems.

    Your team
  2. 2

    We build your digital map

    We map aisles, shelves, checkouts and exits, then set up the indoor positioning that suits your store.

    GMIP team
  3. 3

    Launch in store

    Your store appears in the GMIP app. We provide in-store signage, a short staff briefing, and outreach through accessibility organisations.

    Together
  4. 4

    Stay accurate & improve

    Layout and product updates keep directions correct as stock moves. Shopper feedback and support are included.

    Ongoing

No new app for your customers to pay for. Shoppers download GMIP for free, and it recognises your store automatically when they walk in.
